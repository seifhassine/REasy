Qt and Qt for Python

REasy.exe bundles the Qt 6 libraries and Qt for Python (PySide6, shiboken6), unmodified, under the
GNU Lesser General Public License version 3 (LGPL-3.0.txt, which builds on GPL-3.0.txt).
Qt is a registered trademark of The Qt Company Ltd.

Qt source: https://download.qt.io/official_releases/qt/
Qt for Python source: https://download.qt.io/official_releases/QtForPython/
Licenses of third-party code inside Qt: https://doc.qt.io/qt-6/licenses-used-in-qt.html

REasy's source and its build script (build.bat) are at https://github.com/seifhassine/REasy,
so REasy.exe can be rebuilt with a modified Qt.
